# Relay-owned recovery

Send directly to `server:workspace:tab`, `:workspace:tab`, or `:tab`. The relay
resolves live Herdr topology and enrolls a session-verified recipient that is
missing from its registry. Agents do not need to invite the recipient to join
or manually forward the message through Herdr.

A definite syntax, ambiguity, or missing-session rejection enqueues nothing.
Report its exact cause. Multiple leads in one tab need separate tabs. Missing
Herdr session evidence needs the genuine startup integration repaired; never
substitute a guessed pane or session ID. Codex terminal sessions use the
Herdr launcher with `--no-daemon` and the Herdr session hook before the relay
hook. Preserve `AGENT_MSG_NAME` as environment metadata.

`queued for` is successful enqueue. A timeout has an unknown outcome: inspect
the log or obtain an acknowledgement before retrying. Do not duplicate a
queued message through Herdr.
