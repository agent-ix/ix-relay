# TM-001: ix-relay test matrix

## Overview

One planned test case per obligation (acceptance criterion or NFR measurement
row), keyed on the obligation id: `TC-<FR number>-<AC number>`, and
`TC-N<NFR number>-M-<row>` for measurement rows. **No test exists for a row
unless its status names one** — every other status is 🚧, and those Test IDs are still
slots (AGE-2029's evidence stage wrote the rows marked ✅; a 🚧 partial row names what exists and what does not).
Where a status cites an "existing untagged" test, that test predates the `Tracing:` convention and is cited by name only. `Type` is the evidence kind chosen
from the `quoin advise` recommendation, with the judgement overrides recorded in
[the evidence review](./reviews/evidence.md). Rows marked "red on main" belong to
requirements that `main` does not yet meet (spec.md section 12) and are expected
to fail until the named ticket lands; the ticket is given on each such row.
